# Framewell

Direction selected by the user: a dark editing studio with a warm orange accent. Use shadcn/ui from the Style UI catalog, https://style.ai.id/ui-components.

Reading this as: a video export workspace for everyday creators, in a dark studio visual language, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

Photo addition: keep the same studio direction and dials. A Video/Photo tab changes the media workflow without changing the navigation. Photos use the same portrait preview and inspector; two destination choices reflect the requested WhatsApp Status and Instagram Stories scope. The composition guide is an editing aid, not an official platform safe-area specification. Black and white background choices are functional options for letterboxing and transparency, rather than decorative blur.

- Color: charcoal surfaces keep the video as the visual focus; burnt orange marks selection and the export action. Off-white body copy and warm gray secondary copy provide readable contrast.
- Layout: a narrow utility rail, an open preview stage, and an export inspector mirror the actual pick, inspect, export workflow. On phones the rail becomes a compact header and the inspector follows the preview.
- Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans has a clear, human editorial voice suitable for a creator tool; small native monospace numerals are reserved for time and media measurements.
- Spacing: generous stage space makes room for a vertical preview, while the denser inspector keeps related export choices together.
- Surfaces: two bordered workflow panels contain controls, not marketing feature cards. A raised dialog is the only shadowed overlay.
- Identity: repeated open crop corners frame the empty preview and workspace wordmark area; the product name itself stays text rather than an unapproved logo.
- Icons: Phosphor's compact video, upload, speaker, crop and download symbols describe their actions; no decorative AI glyphs. Platform names remain text with letter labels, not fabricated brand assets.
- Motion: short hover and selection transitions acknowledge interaction. Media playback has controls; all optional motion respects reduced motion.
- Content: no testimonials, customer counts, or guarantees. Presets are this tool's starting points, not official platform specifications. WhatsApp's documented Status compression limitation appears in the guide and selected preset.
- Implementation: official shadcn/ui button, tabs, switch and dialog source adapted to this palette and 44px touch targets; no theme toggle because the user explicitly chose a fixed dark studio.
